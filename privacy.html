Privacy policy

Who we are

Useful Small Systems makes spreadsheets and printables sold through Payhip and shares them on Instagram, TikTok and Pinterest. This page covers our social media publishing tools and this website.

What we collect

This website sets no cookies and collects no personal data. Purchases are handled by Payhip under its own privacy policy. Our publishing tools post our own content to our own social accounts; they do not read, store or process any other person's data, messages or profile information.

Social platform data

When a platform (Meta, TikTok, Pinterest) gives our tools an access token, it is stored only on our own computer, used only to publish our own posts and read our own post statistics, and never shared. We can revoke it at any time from the platform's settings.

E-mail

If you tick the consent box at checkout, Payhip passes your e-mail address to our mailing-list provider so we can send occasional product e-mails. Every e-mail has an unsubscribe link.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, or complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office. E-mail us and we will reply within 30 days.
