CC Ventures

Small, honest utility apps for the UK

CC Ventures builds Android apps that keep your records on your own device: no accounts, no servers, no tracking. Each app is free to use; a one-time Pro unlock removes a limit.

Apps

  • FieldProof: before-and-after photo reports for trades, exported as PDF. Privacy policy
  • Quiet Water: a private fishing catch and spot log with CSV export. Privacy policy
  • Dig Ledger: metal-detecting permissions, finds and an offline scheduled-monument check for England. Privacy policy
  • Claim Ledger: the invoice schedule for a UK self-build VAT reclaim (HMRC VAT431NB), as CSV and PDF. Privacy policy
  • Attorney Account: the records a property and financial affairs attorney keeps under a UK lasting power of attorney, as an account for any period. Privacy policy · Free account template
