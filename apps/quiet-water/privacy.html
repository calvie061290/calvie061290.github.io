Privacy policy

Quiet Water

Effective: 16 October 2026 · Applies to Quiet Water version 0.1.x on Android and the web.

Short version: Quiet Water does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores, and where

Quiet Water saves only on your own device:

  • your catches: species, length, weight, bait, notes and date/time;
  • photos you choose to attach;
  • the private spots you create, including GPS coordinates if you use "Use current location".

There are no accounts, no servers and no cloud sync. The app makes no network requests.

Location

Location is used only when you tap "Use current location" while saving a spot. The coordinates are rounded and saved on your device. They are never sent anywhere. The app does not use background location. You can deny location and still save spots by name.

Photos

Photos are used only when you choose one. A copy is kept inside the app's private storage on your device.

Analytics

The app keeps a small local log of feature events, such as "catch saved", to help a future version improve. The log stays on your device and is never transmitted. The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

Export and deletion

  • You can export all your catches as a CSV file at any time, for free. The file goes wherever you choose to share it.
  • Deleting a catch or spot removes it from the app's storage.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes all of its data permanently. Because the developer never receives your data, the developer cannot recover it.

Backups

Android cloud backup is turned off for this app, so your data is never copied to your Google account. Uninstalling the app deletes your data, so export anything you want to keep first.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds any feature that sends data off your device (for example, optional backup), this policy will be updated first. The feature will be opt-in.
