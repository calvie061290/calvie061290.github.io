Privacy policy

Dig Ledger

Effective: 16 October 2026 · Applies to Dig Ledger version 0.1.x on Android and the web.

Short version: Dig Ledger does not collect, transmit or sell any data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores, and where

Dig Ledger saves the following only on your own device:

  • Permission records: site name, area, dates, conditions, finds agreement, the landowner's name, any contact details you choose to add, and the signatures drawn on screen.
  • Finds: description, category, period, material, depth, date, find spot (GPS coordinates and the Ordnance Survey grid reference), the linked permission, photos you attach, and reporting dates.
  • Settings: your region and the name you enter for agreements.

There are no accounts, no servers and no cloud sync. The app makes no network requests.

Landowners' details

You enter landowners' names, contact details and signatures to keep a record of your agreement with them. Only record details the landowner is happy for you to keep. The developer never receives them.

Location

Location is used only when you tap Check my location or Use my location. It is used to check the position against the scheduled-monument data on your device, and to record a find spot. It is never sent anywhere. The app does not use background location. You can deny location and type a grid reference instead.

Photos

Photos are used only when you choose one. A copy is kept in the app's private storage on your device.

Analytics

The app keeps a small local log of feature events (for example "find saved") that stays on your device and is never transmitted. There are no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

Sharing, export and deletion

  • Permission PDFs and finds CSV files leave the app only when you share or save them yourself.
  • Deleting a record removes it from the app's storage.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes everything permanently. The developer cannot recover it.

Monument data

When monument data is installed, the app bundles official scheduled-monument boundaries for offline checks:

  • England: Historic England data via planning.data.gov.uk.
  • Scotland: Historic Environment Scotland data, where included.

Both are under the Open Government Licence. Regions the data does not cover (currently Wales and Northern Ireland) show as "not covered", never as "none found". The app shows the data's source, date and attribution. Checking a spot uses your location on the device only. A monument check is a reminder, not permission or legal advice.

Backups

Android cloud backup is turned off for this app, so your data is never copied to your Google account. Uninstalling the app deletes your data, so export anything you want to keep first.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds any feature that sends data off your device (for example, optional backup), this policy will be updated first, and the feature will be opt-in.
