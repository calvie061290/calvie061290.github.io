Privacy policy

FieldProof

Effective: 16 October 2026 · Applies to FieldProof version 0.1.x on Android.

Short version: FieldProof does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. Your projects, photos, notes and signatures stay on your device.

What the app stores, and where

FieldProof saves only on your own device:

  • projects: name, address and the company name you enter;
  • report items with their notes and tags;
  • photos you take or choose, and the signature you draw;
  • the PDF reports you generate, until you delete them.

There are no accounts, no servers and no cloud sync. The app makes no network requests of its own.

Camera and photos

The camera and photo library are used only when you tap Add Photo. A copy of each chosen photo is kept inside the app's private storage on your device. You can deny the camera permission and still attach photos from your library.

Pro unlock

The one-time Pro unlock is sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy; the app receives only whether the purchase is owned, so it can lift the free export limit and restore the purchase on a new phone.

Analytics

The app keeps a small local log of feature events, such as "report exported", to help a future version improve. The log stays on your device and is never transmitted. There are no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

Export and deletion

  • You can share any report as a PDF at any time. The file goes wherever you choose to share it.
  • Deleting a project removes it and its photos from the app's storage; Clear all data in Settings removes everything.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes its data. Because the developer never receives your data, the developer cannot recover it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds any feature that sends data off your device, this policy will be updated first and the feature will be opt-in.

Backups

Android cloud backup is turned off for this app, so your records are never copied to your Google account. Uninstalling the app deletes your data, so export anything you want to keep first.
