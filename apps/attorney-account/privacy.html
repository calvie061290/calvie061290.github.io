Privacy policy

Attorney Account

Effective: 16 October 2026 · Applies to Attorney Account version 0.1.x on Android and the web.

Short version: Attorney Account does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. Everything you enter about the donor and yourself stays on your device. CC Ventures never sees it.

What the app stores, and where

Attorney Account saves only on your own device:

  • the donor's name, the type of power of attorney, your name and the date you started acting;
  • the entries you record: balances, income, spending, gifts, donations, loans, your expenses, decisions and addresses, with the dates and notes you type;
  • receipt photos you take or choose (a copy is kept in the app's private storage);
  • the time each entry was first recorded on this phone and any later changes to it.

There are no accounts, no servers and no cloud sync. The app makes no network requests of its own.

Camera and photos

The camera and photo library are used only when you tap Take photo or Add receipt photo. You can deny the camera permission and still attach photos from your library, or record an evidence note instead.

Pro unlock

The one-time Pro unlock is sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy; the app receives only whether the purchase is owned, so it can turn on the account PDF and a second donor and restore the purchase on a new phone.

Analytics

The app keeps a small local log of feature events, such as "entry saved" with the kind of entry. It never contains names, amounts or descriptions, stays on your device and is never transmitted. There are no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

Backups, export and deletion

  • Android cloud backup is turned off for this app, so the donor's records are never copied to a Google account. The backup you export from Settings is the only other copy; keep it somewhere safe. It holds the text of every entry, not the photos.
  • You can export the account as a CSV file at any time, for free. The file goes wherever you choose to share it.
  • Deleting an entry removes it and the app's copy of its receipt photo. Deleting a donor removes all of their entries and photo copies. A photo picked from your gallery stays in the gallery.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes everything permanently. Because the developer never receives your data, the developer cannot recover it.

The donor's data

The records concern another person, the donor. They stay on your device under your control; CC Ventures does not receive, store or process them. Keeping them is part of your duties as an attorney (see GOV.UK, Manage a lasting power of attorney). If you email support, please do not include the donor's details; we will never ask for them.

Not legal advice, not a government service

Attorney Account is a record-keeping tool. It never says whether a gift, loan, expense or decision was right. It is not connected to the Office of the Public Guardian, the Court of Protection or any government body. Information about attorney duties in the app comes from GOV.UK.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds any feature that sends data off your device, this policy will be updated first and the feature will be opt-in.
