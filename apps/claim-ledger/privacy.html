Privacy policy

Claim Ledger

Last updated: 16 October 2026. Claim Ledger is published by CC Ventures (support: CCventures243@gmail.com).

The short version

Claim Ledger keeps your self-build invoice records on your phone. It has no account, no cloud, no advertising and no analytics service. Nothing you type or photograph is sent to us or to anyone else unless you choose to share an export.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your project details: project name, building address, claimant name and certified completion date, if you enter them.
  • Invoice records: supplier name, VAT registration number, invoice number, date, description, totals, VAT, whether the invoice is in your name and your reason if not.
  • Invoice photos you take or choose. They are copied into the app's private storage on the device. Photos chosen from your gallery stay in your gallery too.
  • A local log of app events (for example "invoice saved") used only to diagnose problems if you ask us for help. It never leaves the device and is capped at the last 500 events.

Permissions

  • Camera: used only when you tap Take photo. The app never records video or audio.
  • Photos: used only when you tap Add photo to choose an existing image.
  • No location, contacts, microphone or network permission is used for your data.

Exports

CSV and PDF exports are created on your device and handed to the app or person you choose through your phone's share sheet. Where they go after that is between you and that app.

Pro purchase

The optional one-time Pro unlock is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment and tells the app whether your Google account owns the purchase. We never see your card details. Refunds follow Google Play's policy.

Backups and deletion

Android device backup is switched off for this app, so your records are not copied to Google's backup service. Deleting an invoice removes it from the app at once. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.

Not tax advice

Claim Ledger is a record-keeping tool laid out like HMRC's form VAT431NB. It does not decide what you can claim. HMRC's guidance is at gov.uk/vat-building-new-home. You are responsible for your claim.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
